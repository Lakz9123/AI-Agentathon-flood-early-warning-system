About Sentinel AI: Autonomous Flood Defense Architecture

Predict. Prevent. Protect.

Sentinel AI is a distributed, agentic early-warning ecosystem that eliminates flood response latency. By continuously orchestrating specialized AI micro-agents across satellite meteorological feeds, IoT sensor networks, crowdsourced citizen reports, and historical hydrological databases, Sentinel autonomously synthesizes early flood risks and proposes verified mitigation strategies to municipal authorities before floodwaters breach critical thresholds.
